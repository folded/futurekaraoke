<div class="page-head">
  <h1>About Future Karaoke</h1>
</div>

<div class="prose reveal">

Future Karaoke is a multi-disciplinary performance night dedicated to
showcasing creative talent. It's a uniquely supportive and experimental
platform for artists and writers to present new work and works-in-progress.
With flexible five-minute slots, Future Karaoke fosters a dynamic and inclusive
community, inviting both first-time performers and seasoned professionals to
take creative risks.

Themes are a launching place for inspiration rather than a constraint.

This is not your typical open mic. We are a laboratory for live performance, a
home for every kind of storyteller, and a celebration of the beautifully
unfinished. Whether you have a polished piece or a work-in-progress, this is the
space to test your material on a warm, enthusiastic audience.

Join our community. Sign up for a slot or come be a friendly face in the crowd.

</div>
